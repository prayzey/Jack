# Jack — Brand & Vision

## The Idea

Jack is the **jack-of-all-trades** — one local-first macOS app that pulls together the best ideas and content from across the open source ecosystem into a single, cohesive platform.

Instead of running ten different tools (clipboard manager, dictation, meeting transcription, notes, AI chat, etc.), you get one app that does all of it well, on your own machine.

## Core Principles

1. **All-in-one** — clipboard, dictation, meetings, notes, facts/memory, AI chat, and more, unified under one roof. No tab-switching between five apps to get through a workday.

2. **Local-first** — your data lives on your Mac. Transcription runs locally (Whisper), models cache to disk, notes are plain files. The cloud is optional, not required.

3. **Borrowed brilliance, integrated taste** — Jack stands on the shoulders of great open source projects. We take the good ideas (lil-agents-style patrol, Tolaria-flavored notes, Whisper transcription, Antinote-inspired flows, etc.), credit them honestly, and weave them into something that feels like one product — not a Frankenstein of plugins.

4. **One better thing** — the value isn't in any single feature; it's in the seams. Clipboard items flow into notes. Meetings produce facts. Dictation learns your corrections. Everything talks to everything.

5. **Mac-native craft** — SwiftUI, AppKit where it matters, real animations, real keyboard shortcuts. It should feel like an Apple app, not a cross-platform web wrapper.

## What Jack Is Not

- Not a SaaS — no mandatory account, no per-seat pricing for the core experience.
- Not a thin wrapper around one model or API — the AI is a feature, not the product.
- Not a "kitchen sink" with disconnected tools — every module must justify why it lives inside Jack instead of being a separate app.

## The North Star

A solo professional opens Jack in the morning and closes it at night. In between, it handled their clipboard, their notes, their meeting transcripts, their dictation, and their quick AI questions — without ever needing to leave the app or send their data to someone else's server.

That's Jack.
